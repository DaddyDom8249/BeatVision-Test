# BeatVision Provider Brief

## The opportunity

BeatVision is an AI music-visualization platform built around a simple idea: **Every Song Has a World. BeatVision Reveals It.**

Instead of asking creators to assemble disconnected AI tools, BeatVision turns a song into a structured creative pipeline:

**Song → Visual World → Characters & Environments → Storyboard → Scene Direction → Visual Scenes → Motion → Music Video**

## Why providers matter

BeatVision is deliberately provider-neutral. The platform can route different jobs to specialized providers while keeping the creator experience unified.

### Language / reasoning partner

Power lyrics interpretation, emotional arc extraction, visual-world creation, story beats, continuity notes and scene direction.

### Image partner

Power style frames, character sheets, environments and scene imagery. Reference images can become structured inputs where supported.

### Video partner

Power image-to-video, camera motion, temporal consistency, scene animation and high-quality rendering.

### Infrastructure partner

Power long-running generation jobs, asset storage, render queues, bandwidth and media delivery.

## What we are requesting

BeatVision is seeking one or more partners willing to provide:

- API credits
- Startup / creator program access
- Compute or rendering capacity
- Storage and bandwidth
- Technical partnership
- Product credits for a public prototype demonstration

## What the partner receives

The prototype is designed to make provider integration visible and understandable. A partner can demonstrate its technology inside a real creative workflow rather than a generic benchmark.

Potential partnership placement can include provider-specific demo modes, technical acknowledgements, case-study material, and a documented integration path.

## Current prototype position

The BeatVision-Test repository is intentionally a sponsor presentation build. It uses deterministic presentation outputs when live provider credentials are unavailable, so the demonstration does not depend on spending money just to prove the product concept.

That distinction matters: **the UI demonstrates the intended product; live provider integrations demonstrate the partner's capability.**

## Contact / next step

The repository owner can use this brief alongside the live prototype when approaching model, image, video, infrastructure and startup-program teams.
