# BeatVision — Sponsor Handoff

## What this repository is

BeatVision-Test is a presentation prototype for technology providers, startup programs, and infrastructure partners.

It demonstrates the intended creative orchestration without requiring credentials or making paid API calls.

## Five-minute presentation path

1. **The Song** — show that the creator supplies a song and intent, not an engineering prompt.
2. **Reveal the World** — demonstrate the visual-world report and the creator approval gate: “Yes, that’s my world.”
3. **World Assets** — show how the approved world becomes reusable characters, environments, and style rules.
4. **Storyboard** — show the song becoming a sequence of production-ready visual beats.
5. **Scene Direction** — explain how approved creative state becomes provider-ready instructions.
6. **Visual Scenes** — show the image-generation handoff and reference-input model.
7. **Motion & Edit** — show where an image-to-video provider supplies motion and temporal consistency.
8. **The Pitch** — map the workflow directly to the partner’s capability and the requested support.

## Partnership surface

BeatVision is intentionally provider-neutral. A partner can contribute one capability or multiple capabilities:

- **Language & reasoning:** lyrics interpretation, emotional arc, world building, continuity, scene direction.
- **Image generation:** characters, environments, style frames, scene imagery, reference-aware generation.
- **Video generation:** image-to-video, camera motion, temporal consistency, rendering.
- **Audio intelligence:** tempo, sections, energy, beat and musical-event analysis.
- **Infrastructure:** jobs, compute, storage, queues, bandwidth and media delivery.

## What BeatVision is asking for

- API or inference credits
- Startup / creator program access
- Compute or render capacity
- Storage and bandwidth
- Technical partnership
- Strategic sponsorship

## What a technology partner receives

- A concrete creative-product integration surface
- Provider-specific demonstration modes
- Technical acknowledgement and partnership visibility
- A documented path from prototype to live adapter
- Potential case-study and launch collaboration opportunities

## Important prototype boundary

The current demo deliberately uses deterministic presentation outputs. It does **not** claim that a third-party model generated the displayed results.

Live providers can replace the demo adapters behind the capability interfaces documented in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `SPONSOR_INTEGRATION.md`.
